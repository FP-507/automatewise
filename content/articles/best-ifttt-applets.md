## What Makes a Great IFTTT Applet

A great applet saves you real time on a task you actually do repeatedly. The best ones are set-and-forget: you configure them once and they work in the background indefinitely. We tested hundreds of applets and selected these 25 based on reliability, practical value, and ease of setup.

If you are new to IFTTT, start with our [getting started guide](/blog/getting-started-with-ifttt) to understand triggers, actions, and how to connect your accounts. Each applet below takes under two minutes to configure.

## Productivity Applets (1-8)

### 1. Log Work Hours to Google Sheets

**Trigger:** Button widget press (start/stop)
**Action:** Add row to Google Sheets with timestamp

Track your work hours by pressing a button when you start and stop working. IFTTT logs each press with the exact time to a spreadsheet. At the end of the week, your timesheet is already built. Freelancers and consultants use this to track billable hours without dedicated time-tracking software.

### 2. Save Interesting Articles to Read Later

**Trigger:** Like a tweet or save a Reddit post
**Action:** Add to Pocket, Instapaper, or a Notion database

Every time you like a tweet that links to an article, IFTTT automatically saves it to your read-later app. Works with Twitter/X, Reddit saves, and YouTube likes. No more losing interesting content in infinite scroll.

### 3. Daily Weather Briefing

**Trigger:** Every day at 7:00 AM
**Action:** Send notification with today's weather forecast

Get a push notification every morning with the temperature, conditions, and rain probability for your location. Simpler and faster than opening a weather app. Uses IFTTT's Weather Underground integration for accurate local forecasts.

### 4. Email Attachments to Cloud Storage

**Trigger:** New email with attachment in Gmail
**Action:** Save attachment to Google Drive or Dropbox

Every email attachment automatically appears in a designated folder in your cloud storage. Contracts, invoices, photos -- everything is filed without manual downloading. Set up folder rules in your cloud storage for further organization. For more Gmail automations, see our [Google Workspace automation guide](/blog/automate-google-workspace).

### 5. Meeting Reminder with Prep Notes

**Trigger:** Google Calendar event starting in 15 minutes
**Action:** Send notification with event details and notes

Get a rich notification before each meeting that includes the event title, location, attendees, and any notes you added to the calendar event. More useful than the default calendar reminder because it surfaces the context you need to prepare. Pairs well with our guide on [automating calendar management](/blog/automate-calendar-management).

### 6. Track Package Deliveries

**Trigger:** Email from shipping carriers (UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL)
**Action:** Add tracking info to a Google Sheet

IFTTT filters your email for shipping notifications and logs the tracking number, carrier, and expected delivery date to a spreadsheet. During holiday season or for businesses receiving frequent shipments, this creates a simple delivery dashboard without paid tracking software.

### 7. Automatic Do Not Disturb

**Trigger:** Google Calendar event starts (meetings, focus time)
**Action:** Enable Do Not Disturb on your Android phone

When a calendar event begins, your phone automatically silences. When the event ends, notifications resume. Tag focus time blocks in your calendar and your phone enforces them. iOS has built-in Focus modes but this gives you calendar-linked control on Android.

### 8. Daily Journal Prompt

**Trigger:** Every day at 9:00 PM
**Action:** Create a note in Evernote, Google Docs, or Day One

IFTTT sends you a daily prompt and creates a pre-formatted journal entry with the date, day of the week, and template questions. Building a journaling habit is easier when the page is already open and waiting.

## Smart Home Applets (9-16)

### 9. Sunset Lights On

**Trigger:** Sunset at your location
**Action:** Turn on living room smart lights

No timers to adjust seasonally. IFTTT calculates sunset based on your GPS coordinates and triggers lights at the exact right time year-round. Works with Philips Hue, LIFX, Wyze, TP-Link Kasa, and most WiFi bulbs. See our complete [IFTTT smart home guide](/blog/ifttt-smart-home-automation) for more lighting automations.

### 10. Leave Home, Lock Up

**Trigger:** You exit a geographic area around your home
**Action:** Lock smart lock, arm cameras, set thermostat to eco

The single most valuable smart home applet. Eliminates the "did I lock the door?" anxiety. IFTTT detects you have left based on your phone's location and secures everything. Requires IFTTT Pro for multiple actions from one trigger.

### 11. Doorbell Camera to Slack or Email

**Trigger:** Ring or Nest doorbell pressed
**Action:** Send photo notification to Slack, email, or phone

Get a snapshot from your doorbell camera the moment someone presses it. Useful if you work from home and want to decide whether to answer without getting up, or if you are away and want to see who visited.

### 12. Garage Door Safety Net

**Trigger:** Every day at 11:00 PM
**Action:** Check garage door status and close if open

If your smart garage opener supports IFTTT (Chamberlain MyQ, Tailwind, or a SmartThings-connected sensor), this nightly check ensures you never leave the garage open overnight. Simple but prevents a surprisingly common security lapse.

### 13. Weather Alert Preparation

**Trigger:** Severe weather alert for your area
**Action:** Turn on all lights, send notification, adjust thermostat

When the National Weather Service issues a severe weather alert for your location, IFTTT turns on lights so you can see, sends a detailed notification, and pre-adjusts your thermostat in case of power fluctuation.

### 14. Robot Vacuum on Schedule When Away

**Trigger:** You leave home (location)
**Action:** Start iRobot Roomba or Roborock vacuum

Your robot vacuum runs while you are out, so you return to clean floors without hearing the vacuum. Pairs the location trigger with IFTTT's iRobot or Roborock integration. Better than a fixed schedule because it adapts to when you actually leave.

### 15. Smart Plug Energy Monitor

**Trigger:** Every day at midnight
**Action:** Log smart plug energy usage to Google Sheets

If your smart plugs report energy consumption (TP-Link Kasa, Wemo), log daily usage to a spreadsheet. Over a month, you can see which devices consume the most power and identify energy savings opportunities.

### 16. Bedtime Wind-Down

**Trigger:** Every day at 10:00 PM
**Action:** Dim lights to 20%, set warm tone, lower thermostat 2 degrees

Gradual environmental changes that signal your body it is time to sleep. Research shows dimming lights and cooling the bedroom improves sleep quality. This applet automates the transition without you thinking about it.

## Social Media Applets (17-21)

### 17. Cross-Post Instagram to Twitter

**Trigger:** New photo posted on Instagram
**Action:** Post the image as a native Twitter photo (not a link)

Instagram posts shared to Twitter natively only post a link, which gets terrible engagement. This IFTTT applet posts the actual image to Twitter with your caption, getting much better visibility and engagement. For a complete social strategy, see our [social media automation guide](/blog/automate-social-media-posting).

### 18. Save All Your Social Mentions

**Trigger:** New mention on Twitter, Reddit, or YouTube
**Action:** Add to a Google Sheet with link and timestamp

Track every time someone mentions you or your brand across platforms. Instead of checking each platform individually, review one spreadsheet. Useful for brand monitoring, customer support, and identifying engagement opportunities.

### 19. Auto-Share Blog Posts

**Trigger:** New item in RSS feed (your blog)
**Action:** Post to Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, or Pinterest

Every new blog post you publish automatically gets shared to your social channels. Add your blog's RSS feed URL and IFTTT handles distribution. For more sophisticated content distribution, consider [email marketing automation](/blog/automate-email-marketing).

### 20. YouTube to Podcast Cross-Promotion

**Trigger:** New video uploaded to your YouTube channel
**Action:** Tweet about it, post to Facebook, add to a spreadsheet

Content creators benefit from automatic cross-promotion. The moment your new video goes live on YouTube, IFTTT announces it everywhere. Track all your uploads in a spreadsheet for content calendar management. More creator automations in our [content creators guide](/blog/automation-for-content-creators).

### 21. Curate Content Automatically

**Trigger:** New post in a specific subreddit, RSS feed, or hashtag
**Action:** Save to Pocket, email digest, or spreadsheet

Follow topics relevant to your industry by monitoring specific sources. IFTTT watches subreddits, hashtags, or RSS feeds and saves matching content. Great for content curation, competitive research, or staying current in your field without manual browsing.

## Notification and Communication Applets (22-25)

### 22. Custom Slack Notifications from Anywhere

**Trigger:** Any IFTTT trigger (email, calendar, smart home, weather)
**Action:** Post message to a Slack channel

Route important notifications from any service into Slack. New customer email? Slack. Server down? Slack. Package delivered? Slack. Centralizes your notifications into the tool you already watch all day. For more, see our [Slack automation guide](/blog/automate-slack-notifications).

### 23. Emergency Contact Alert

**Trigger:** Button widget press
**Action:** Send SMS to emergency contact with your GPS location

Create a safety button that sends your exact location to a trusted contact. One tap when you feel unsafe, and help knows where you are. Keep the IFTTT widget on your phone's home screen for quick access.

### 24. Price Drop Alerts

**Trigger:** RSS feed from price tracking sites (CamelCamelCamel, Honey)
**Action:** Send notification with product and new price

Monitor price drops on products you want to buy. Connect RSS feeds from price tracking services and get notified the moment a price drops below your target. Saves money without manually checking prices daily.

### 25. Daily Digest of Everything

**Trigger:** Every day at 8:00 AM
**Action:** Send email with summary of yesterday's automations

IFTTT can email you a digest of everything it did in the past 24 hours. How many applets ran, which ones triggered, and whether any failed. A quick daily health check for your automation system, similar to building an [automation monitoring dashboard](/blog/build-automation-dashboard).

## Free vs Pro: Which Applets Need IFTTT Pro?

IFTTT's free plan allows 2 active applets with single trigger-action pairs. IFTTT Pro ($3.49/month) unlocks unlimited applets and multi-step applets with multiple actions, queries, and conditional logic.

**Free plan can handle:** Any single trigger to single action applet. Pick two from this list that save you the most time.

**Pro is worth it if:** You want three or more applets active, or need multi-step routines (morning routine, away routine, smart home sequences). At $3.49/month, it is the cheapest automation subscription available -- see our [best free automation tools guide](/blog/best-free-automation-tools) for comparison.

## Setting Up Your First Applet

1. Go to **ifttt.com** and sign in (or [create an account](/blog/getting-started-with-ifttt))
2. Click "Create" in the top menu
3. Click "If This" and choose your trigger service
4. Connect your account for that service and select the trigger event
5. Click "Then That" and choose your action service
6. Connect the action account and configure the action
7. Name your applet and click "Finish"

The applet runs immediately. Test it by triggering the condition manually (press the button, send a test email, etc.) and verify the action fires correctly.
