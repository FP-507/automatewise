## Why Small Businesses Should Start with IFTTT

IFTTT is not typically mentioned alongside enterprise automation platforms, and that is exactly why it works for small businesses. At $3.49/month for unlimited applets, it costs a fraction of what [Zapier](/blog/getting-started-with-zapier) ($19.99/month) or [Make](/blog/getting-started-with-make) ($9/month) charge for comparable usage.

The tradeoff is simplicity. IFTTT handles straightforward trigger-action automations well but lacks the complex logic, data transformation, and multi-branch workflows that larger tools provide. For a business running 5-20 automations that follow simple if-this-then-that patterns, IFTTT delivers 80% of the value at 15% of the cost.

If you are evaluating options, our [best automation tools for small businesses](/blog/best-automation-tools-small-business) guide compares all platforms at different price points. New to IFTTT? Start with our [getting started guide](/blog/getting-started-with-ifttt).

## Marketing Automations

### Lead Capture to Spreadsheet

**Trigger:** New form submission (Google Forms, Typeform, JotForm)
**Action:** Add lead details to a Google Sheet and send a notification

Every form submission on your website automatically lands in a central spreadsheet with name, email, source, and timestamp. You get a push notification so you can follow up quickly. For businesses not ready for a full CRM, a well-organized Google Sheet works surprisingly well as a lead database.

For more sophisticated form handling, see our [Google Forms automation guide](/blog/google-forms-automation).

### Social Media Monitoring

**Trigger:** New mention of your brand name on Twitter or Reddit
**Action:** Log to spreadsheet and send Slack notification

Track when people talk about your business online without paying for social listening tools. IFTTT watches for specific keywords (your brand name, product name, or industry terms) and alerts you in real time. Respond quickly to customer feedback, complaints, or opportunities.

### Content Distribution

**Trigger:** New blog post published (RSS feed)
**Action:** Share to Twitter, Facebook page, LinkedIn

Stop manually sharing every blog post to every platform. Add your blog's RSS feed URL and IFTTT publishes to all your social channels automatically. The post goes out with your title and link -- customize the template for each platform's format.

For more comprehensive social strategies, see our [social media posting automation guide](/blog/automate-social-media-posting).

### Email List Building

**Trigger:** New row in Google Sheet (from lead capture)
**Action:** Add subscriber to Mailchimp, ConvertKit, or Sendinblue

When a new lead enters your spreadsheet, IFTTT adds them to your email marketing list automatically. No manual importing, no CSV uploads. Combine with [email marketing automation](/blog/automate-email-marketing) for a complete funnel from capture to nurture.

### Review Monitoring

**Trigger:** New Google Business review or Yelp review
**Action:** Send notification and log to spreadsheet

Get alerted the moment a new review appears so you can respond quickly. Quick responses to reviews (especially negative ones) demonstrate that you care about customer experience. Track review sentiment over time in a spreadsheet.

## Sales Automations

### New Customer Welcome Sequence

**Trigger:** New sale on Shopify, WooCommerce, or Stripe
**Action:** Send thank-you email, add to CRM, notify sales team

When a purchase comes through, IFTTT triggers a chain of actions: the customer gets a personalized thank-you email, their information is added to your CRM or spreadsheet, and your team gets notified. For [more complete onboarding workflows](/blog/automate-customer-onboarding), consider a dedicated platform.

### Pipeline Updates

**Trigger:** Deal stage changes in your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive)
**Action:** Post update to Slack and log to spreadsheet

Keep your team informed about sales progress without checking the CRM constantly. When a deal moves to a new stage, IFTTT posts an update to your sales channel. For deeper CRM automation, see our [CRM automation guide](/blog/automate-crm-workflows).

### Meeting Booked Notification

**Trigger:** New booking on Calendly or Cal.com
**Action:** Send team notification, add to CRM, create follow-up task

When a prospect books a meeting, everyone who needs to know gets notified instantly. The meeting details are logged to your CRM and a follow-up task is created. See our [appointment scheduling automation guide](/blog/automate-appointment-scheduling) for the full booking-to-follow-up workflow.

### Lost Deal Analysis

**Trigger:** Deal marked as lost in CRM
**Action:** Add to lost deals spreadsheet with reason and value

Track why deals fail. When a deal is marked lost, IFTTT captures the reason, deal value, stage where it was lost, and the date. Over time, patterns emerge: pricing too high, feature gaps, slow response times. Data-driven insights for improving your close rate.

## Operations Automations

### Team Attendance Tracking

**Trigger:** Button widget press (clock in / clock out)
**Action:** Log timestamp and employee name to Google Sheet

Simple time tracking for small teams. Each employee presses a button widget on their phone when arriving and leaving. IFTTT logs the times to a shared spreadsheet. Works for remote teams too -- the timestamp proves when someone started working. No expensive time-tracking software needed.

### Inventory Alerts

**Trigger:** Spreadsheet value drops below threshold
**Action:** Send notification and email to supplier

If you track inventory in a spreadsheet (many small businesses do), IFTTT can monitor specific cells. When stock drops below your reorder point, you get an alert and can optionally send a pre-written reorder email to your supplier. For [full inventory management automation](/blog/automate-inventory-management), dedicated tools handle this better at scale.

### Customer Feedback Collection

**Trigger:** X days after purchase (date-based)
**Action:** Send feedback request email

Schedule follow-up emails asking for feedback or reviews at the right moment -- not immediately (customer hasn't used the product yet) and not too late (they have forgotten the experience). IFTTT's date triggers let you time these precisely. More strategies in our [customer feedback automation guide](/blog/automate-customer-feedback).

### Daily Business Digest

**Trigger:** Every morning at 8:00 AM
**Action:** Email summary of yesterday's key metrics

Compile yesterday's new leads, sales, support tickets, and social mentions into one email. IFTTT pulls from your connected services and delivers a morning briefing so you start the day knowing where things stand. For more detailed reporting, see our [automated report generation guide](/blog/automate-report-generation).

## IFTTT Pro vs Free for Business Use

The free plan's 2-applet limit is not enough for business use. At $3.49/month, IFTTT Pro gives you:

- **Unlimited applets** -- Run all the automations in this article simultaneously
- **Multi-step applets** -- One trigger fires multiple actions (lead captured → add to sheet AND send notification AND add to email list)
- **Conditional logic** -- Add filters so actions only run when conditions are met (only notify for leads from specific sources)
- **Faster polling** -- Check for new triggers more frequently

At $42/year, IFTTT Pro is the most affordable automation subscription for small businesses. Compare this to [Zapier at $240/year](/blog/getting-started-with-zapier) or [Make at $108/year](/blog/getting-started-with-make) for their starter plans.

## When to Outgrow IFTTT for Business

IFTTT serves small businesses well until you hit these walls:

**Need for complex logic** -- Multi-branch workflows, loops, error handling, and data transformation require [Make](/blog/getting-started-with-make) or [n8n](/blog/getting-started-with-n8n).

**High volume** -- Processing hundreds of events per hour benefits from platforms with better throughput and queueing.

**Team features** -- Multiple users, role-based access, shared workflow libraries, and audit logs are enterprise features found in [Power Automate](/blog/getting-started-with-power-automate) or [n8n](/blog/getting-started-with-n8n).

**Advanced integrations** -- Need to connect to databases, custom APIs, or internal tools? [Zapier](/blog/getting-started-with-zapier) (7,000+ integrations) or n8n (400+ with custom code nodes) handle this better.

The smart path: start with IFTTT for immediate wins, then migrate specific workflows to more powerful platforms as complexity demands. You do not need to move everything at once.
