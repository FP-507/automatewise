## Why Zapier Templates Save You Hours

Zapier's template library contains thousands of pre-built Zaps that connect popular apps in proven patterns. Instead of building automations from scratch, you pick a template, connect your accounts, and it works. Most templates are set up in under 3 minutes.

For small businesses running 5-20 automations, templates cover 80% of needs out of the box. The remaining 20% can be customized by editing the template after installation. This guide highlights the 20 most impactful templates based on time saved and business value.

New to Zapier? Our [getting started guide](/blog/getting-started-with-zapier) walks you through creating your first Zap. For a comparison with other platforms, see our [Make vs Zapier guide](/blog/make-vs-zapier) or [IFTTT vs Zapier vs Make](/blog/ifttt-vs-zapier-vs-make).

## Lead Capture and CRM (1-5)

### 1. Google Forms → Google Sheets + Email Notification

When someone fills out your contact form, the response is added to a tracking spreadsheet and you get an email notification. The most basic and most useful automation for any business collecting leads online. Extend this with our [Google Forms automation guide](/blog/google-forms-automation).

### 2. Facebook Lead Ads → CRM + Welcome Email

Facebook lead form submissions go directly into HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive as new contacts. A welcome email sends automatically. No more downloading CSV files from Facebook Ads Manager and manually importing them. For more lead strategies, see our [lead generation guide](/blog/automate-lead-generation).

### 3. New Email → CRM Contact

When you receive an email from a new address, Zapier creates a contact in your CRM. Your CRM stays populated with everyone you correspond with -- no manual entry. Filter to only process emails from specific domains or with specific subjects to avoid adding every spam sender.

### 4. Calendly Booking → CRM + Slack Notification

When a prospect books a meeting through Calendly, their information is added to your CRM and your team gets a Slack notification with the booking details. The meeting appears in your pipeline before you even check your calendar. More booking patterns in our [appointment scheduling guide](/blog/automate-appointment-scheduling).

### 5. Typeform Survey → Segmented Email Lists

Survey responses are analyzed by Zapier and contacts are added to different email lists based on their answers. Someone interested in product A goes to List A; product B interest goes to List B. Segmented lists get higher open rates than blasting everyone with the same content.

## Email and Communication (6-10)

### 6. Gmail Attachment → Google Drive

Every email attachment automatically saves to a designated Google Drive folder. Contracts, invoices, photos, documents -- all filed without manual downloading. Add subfolders per sender or file type for organization. Part of our broader [Google Workspace automation guide](/blog/automate-google-workspace).

### 7. New Team Member → Welcome Email Sequence

When someone is added to your team Slack workspace or email system, Zapier sends a series of welcome emails over the first week: Day 1 (welcome + first-day info), Day 3 (key tools and resources), Day 7 (check-in and feedback request). See our [HR onboarding guide](/blog/automation-for-hr-onboarding) for the full workflow.

### 8. Customer Support Email → Ticket + Slack Alert

Emails to support@yourcompany.com create a ticket in your helpdesk (Zendesk, Freshdesk, or a Google Sheet) and alert the support team in Slack. Response time drops because the right people know about the ticket immediately. For AI-powered support, see our [customer support chatbot guide](/blog/automate-chatbot-customer-support).

### 9. Scheduled Email Digest

Every Friday at 4 PM, Zapier compiles the week's key metrics (from Google Sheets, CRM, or other sources) into a formatted email sent to your team or stakeholders. Weekly reporting without manual effort. See our [report generation guide](/blog/automate-report-generation).

### 10. Follow-Up Reminder After Meeting

Two days after a Google Calendar meeting, Zapier sends you a reminder to follow up with the attendees. Includes the meeting name and attendee emails so you can act immediately. Our [follow-up email guide](/blog/automate-follow-up-emails) covers automated follow-up sequences.

## Sales and E-Commerce (11-14)

### 11. Shopify Order → Google Sheets + Shipping Notification

Every Shopify order is logged to a spreadsheet with customer details, items, total, and date. The customer gets a confirmation email. Your fulfillment team gets a Slack notification. One Zap replaces three manual steps.

### 12. Stripe Subscription → Onboarding Workflow

When a new subscription starts in Stripe, Zapier creates the customer in your CRM, adds them to an onboarding email sequence, creates a project in your PM tool (Trello, Asana, Notion), and notifies your customer success team. Full [customer onboarding automation](/blog/automate-customer-onboarding) from payment to delivery.

### 13. Abandoned Cart Recovery Email

When a Shopify cart is abandoned, Zapier waits 1 hour and sends a recovery email with the abandoned items and a link back to checkout. Recovers 5-15% of abandoned carts without dedicated cart recovery software. More e-commerce patterns in our [e-commerce automation guide](/blog/automation-for-ecommerce).

### 14. New Review → Social Proof + Response Alert

When a new Google Business or Yelp review is posted, positive reviews (4-5 stars) are automatically shared to social media as testimonials. All reviews trigger a notification so you can respond quickly. Fast response to reviews improves local SEO and customer trust.

## Team Productivity (15-18)

### 15. Slack Message → Task Creation

React to a Slack message with a specific emoji (📋 or ✅) and Zapier creates a task in Trello, Asana, or Notion with the message content. Convert discussions into action items without switching apps. For full project management automation, see our [PM automation guide](/blog/automate-project-management).

### 16. Google Calendar Event → Meeting Notes Template

When a meeting appears on your calendar, Zapier creates a Google Doc with the meeting name, date, attendees, and a structured template for notes and action items. Walk into every meeting with a prepared document. Pairs with our [meeting scheduling guide](/blog/automate-meeting-scheduling).

### 17. Daily Standup Bot

Every weekday at 9 AM, Zapier posts a standup prompt to your team's Slack channel: "What did you accomplish yesterday? What are you working on today? Any blockers?" Responses thread under the prompt, creating an async standup that replaces the daily meeting for remote teams.

### 18. Time-Off Request Workflow

When someone submits a time-off request (via Google Forms or Slack), Zapier adds it to a shared calendar, notifies their manager for approval, and logs it to a tracking spreadsheet. Approved requests update the team calendar automatically.

## Tips for Getting the Most from Templates

**Start with one template.** Install it, run it for a week, and confirm it works reliably before adding more. A single reliable automation is worth more than ten broken ones.

**Read the template before installing.** Check which apps it connects, what triggers it uses, and what actions it takes. Make sure it matches your actual workflow, not just the title.

**Customize the filter.** Most templates process every trigger event. Add Zapier Filters to only run for relevant events (specific form answers, certain email senders, orders above a threshold).

**Monitor your task usage.** Each Zap step that runs counts as one "task" against your Zapier plan. A Zap with 5 steps processing 100 triggers/month uses 500 tasks. The free plan includes 100 tasks/month; Starter includes 750. For higher volumes, compare pricing with [Make](/blog/make-vs-zapier) or [n8n](/blog/n8n-vs-zapier).

**Use Zapier's Formatter.** Between trigger and action, add a Formatter step to clean data: format dates, capitalize names, split full names into first/last, convert currencies. Clean data prevents errors downstream.
